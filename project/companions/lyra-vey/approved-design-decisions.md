# Lyra Vey — Approved Design Decisions

These decisions are approved for the Lyra Vey companion build and constrain the next Character Architect / Rules / Research pass.

## Locked Foundation

- Human · Wizard · Scholar · Level 1
- Role: Arcane Damage / Investigation
- STR 8, DEX 12, CON 10, INT 16, WIS 13, CHA 11
- HP 7, AC 10, Stamina 10, Proficiency +2
- Personality: Curious, driven, analytical
- Values: Knowledge, discovery, truth
- Flaw: Will risk herself for an unanswered question
- Backstory: Scholar who disappeared investigating the Bloodglass Sanctum
- Motivation: Recover her research and discover the truth about awakened bloodlines
- Recruitment: Shadow must rescue her and prove he isn't simply another person hunting the Bloodglass secrets

## Approved Mechanical Direction

1. **Rage scaling:** Tiered damage scaling.
2. **Rage generation:** Risk/reward. Fire damage, taking damage, and critical events can contribute; exact values must be balanced by the assistants.
3. **Rage decay:** Rage represents Lyra's anger. It subsides after conflict when the source of anger is gone; it is not an arbitrary per-turn decay timer.
4. **Rage spending:** Rage can be spent on special abilities.
5. **Fire anchor:** Burning Hands is an approved core fire spell.
6. **Emberborn:** Escalating bloodline awakening tied to Rage thresholds.
7. **Signature ability:** Flame Feedback — being hurt fuels Lyra's Rage.
8. **Investigation:** Hybrid Bloodglass Specialist + Arcane Investigator.
9. **High Rage:** Damage continues scaling and high Rage unlocks optional effects that can consume Rage.
10. **Overheat:** Hard Overheat. Reaching maximum Rage creates a danger when another Rage gain would occur; exact backlash must be designed and balanced.
11. **Long-term identity:** Fire + Rage + Bloodglass + mastery.
12. **Spellcasting:** Spellcrafting. Lyra develops the ability to modify spells using her knowledge and magical/rage systems over progression.
