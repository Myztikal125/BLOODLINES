# Lyra Vey — Character Architect Build Brief

## Status
Companion #1. Approved foundation. Build proposal pending player approval.

## Locked Foundation
- Name: Lyra Vey
- Ancestry: Human
- Class: Wizard
- Background: Scholar
- Level: 1
- Role: Arcane Damage / Investigation
- STR 8, DEX 12, CON 10, INT 16, WIS 13, CHA 11
- HP 7, AC 10, Stamina 10, Proficiency +2
- Personality: Curious, driven, analytical
- Values: Knowledge, discovery, truth
- Flaw: Will risk herself for an unanswered question.
- Backstory: The scholar who disappeared while investigating the Bloodglass Sanctum.
- Motivation: Recover her research and discover the truth about awakened bloodlines.
- Recruitment: Shadow has to rescue her and prove he isn't simply another person hunting the Bloodglass secrets.

## Primary Character Direction
**Fire + Rage** is the defining mechanical/fantasy direction for Lyra.

Do not replace this with a generic arcane, shadow, ice, or unrelated wizard concept. Her approved role remains Arcane Damage / Investigation, but Fire + Rage is the core identity the kit must express.

## Rules Framework
Use all three rulesets as appropriate:

1. BLOODLINES custom rules — highest priority.
2. D&D 2024 — preferred modern baseline where compatible.
3. D&D 2014 — retain compatible/useful mechanics where they better support the design.
4. Hybrid/custom BLOODLINES mechanics are explicitly allowed when required to make the character work.

Every hybrid/custom rule must be clearly identified and justified.

## Important Separation
Clearly label each mechanic as one of:
- EXISTING — already implemented/approved in BLOODLINES
- ADAPTED — based on D&D 2014/2024 and compatible with BLOODLINES
- HYBRID — combines/adapts rules from multiple sources
- PROPOSED — new BLOODLINES mechanic requiring approval/implementation

Do not silently invent mechanics and present them as existing.

## Approval Gate
This is a design proposal, not automatic canon. The final Lyra sheet becomes approved only after player review. Do not modify the locked foundation stats or identity.
