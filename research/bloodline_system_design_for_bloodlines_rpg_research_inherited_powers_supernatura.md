# BLOODLINES RPG: Bloodline System Design Research Notes

## Summary
The Bloodline system in the BLOODLINES RPG serves as a core mechanic that ties character identity and progression directly to their ancestry and supernatural heritage. This system introduces inherited powers, curses, and blessings from their bloodline, while incorporating unique progression systems that allow characters to awaken and develop their abilities over time. The interplay between bloodlines not only enhances individual character capabilities but also contributes dynamically to the game's storytelling.

## Important Mechanics

### 1. Inherited Powers
- **Definition**: Powers passed down from ancestry, providing characters with unique abilities based on their lineage.
- **Mechanics**: 
  - Powers can be categorized into tiers (e.g., common, rare, legendary).
  - Each character begins with a base power and can unlock additional powers as they level up.

### 2. Supernatural Ancestry
- **Types**:
  - Vampire, Werewolf, Fey, Elemental, etc.
  - Each type comes with specific traits, strengths, and weaknesses that affect gameplay.
  
### 3. Progression Systems
- **Experience Points (XP)**: Players earn XP through exploration, combat, and story quests.
- **Bloodline Evolution**: Players choose a pathway for their bloodline’s development, which could branch off at specific levels.
- **Character Levels**: Each level grants more access to bloodline mechanics, such as unlocking new powers or enhancing existing ones.

### 4. Awakening Mechanics
- **Triggers**: Specific events, such as reaching a milestone, overcoming a significant challenge, or a ritual, can lead to an awakening of latent bloodline abilities.
- **Consequences**: Awakening may have both beneficial effects (new abilities) and drawbacks (temporary weaknesses, loss of control).

### 5. Mutations, Curses, and Blessings
- **Mutations**: Random transformations that can both hinder and enhance character abilities, adding an element of unpredictability.
- **Curses**: Negative effects tied to bloodline heritage, which players must learn to mitigate or overcome.
- **Blessings**: Special bonuses that can provide gameplay advantages, such as temporary boosts or immunity from specific status effects.

### 6. Class Interactions
- **Bloodline specific classes**: Class systems that provide synergy with bloodline traits (e.g., a vampire class that enhances stealth and biting powers).
- **Multi-classing**: Ability to select abilities from other bloodlines, enabling players to mix different ancestries for diverse skill sets.

### 7. Character Identity and Storytelling
- **Backstory Integration**: Bloodlines should inform character motivations, ties to NPCs, and personal quests.
- **Social Dynamics**: Characters may face biases or privileges based on their bloodline, affecting in-game interaction.
- **Legacy Quests**: Special story arcs that explore the history of a character's bloodline, allowing them to unlock new powers or resolve ancient issues.

## Design Ideas

1. **Bloodline Trees**: Create a branching tree system that visually represents a player's bloodline evolution, allowing players to choose paths that suit their playstyle.
2. **Destiny Cards**: Introduce cards that specify potential mutations, curses, or blessings, adding randomness and replayability.
3. **Legacy Influences**: Allow players to draw from legendary figures within their ancestry to gain temporary buffs or unique traits during critical moments.
4. **Blood Oath System**: Implement a mechanic where players can make oaths to their bloodline or to other characters, granting bonuses or powering up specific abilities when the oath is honored.

## Possible Implementation Notes

1. **Balance Considerations**: Ensure that no bloodline is inherently superior by carefully limiting powerful abilities and integrating weaknesses.
2. **Testing and Iteration**: Run playtests focused on the bloodline systems specifically to gauge player enjoyment and balance.
3. **Lore Development**: Establish a rich lore connected to various bloodlines with mythological or historical ties to enhance depth and immersion in storytelling.
4. **Player Customization**: Offer players comprehensive options to design their bloodlines or choose from established ones, encouraging personal investment in character creation.

### Conclusion
The Bloodline system presents a myriad of opportunities for enriching character depth, enhancing gameplay dynamics, and fostering engaging narrative experiences. By involving players in their ancestral legacies, BLOODLINES RPG can create a unique game environment that interweaves personal growth with a larger thematic tapestry, allowing for rich storytelling and character development.
