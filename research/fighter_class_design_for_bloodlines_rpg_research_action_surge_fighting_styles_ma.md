# BLOODLINES RPG: Fighter Class Design Research Notes

## Summary
The Fighter class in BLOODLINES RPG is a versatile martial character driven by combat prowess, tactical skill, and physical strength. This class emphasizes a combination of raw fighting capabilities, specialized techniques in combat, and potential integration with bloodline powers that can enhance their martial effectiveness. The Fighter excels in direct confrontations, using their unique features to adapt to various combat scenarios.

## Important Mechanics

1. **Action Surge**: 
   - A core ability allowing Fighters to push their limits and take an additional action in a turn, enhancing their damage output or allowing for tactical repositioning in combat.
   - Can be a limited-use feature that restores after a short or long rest.

2. **Fighting Styles**: 
   - Fighters can choose a fighting style at certain levels that represents their preferred approach to combat (e.g., Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting). 
   - Provides bonuses tailored to their combat preferences, enhancing versatility.

3. **Martial Archetypes**: 
   - Fighters select an archetype at a certain level, allowing them to specialize further (e.g., Champion, Battle Master, Eldritch Knight). 
   - Each archetype brings unique skills, tactics, and potential spells or abilities that can interact with bloodline powers.

4. **Second Wind**: 
   - A self-healing ability that allows a Fighter to regain hit points on their turn (limited use).
   - Ideal for sustaining durability in combat and maintaining pressure on opponents.

5. **Ability Score Improvements (ASI)**: 
   - Fighters have multiple opportunities to improve their ability scores or select feats, enabling them to bolster key attributes such as Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution.
   - Enhancements should reflect their evolving combat style and personal growth.

6. **Weapon Mastery**: 
   - Possibly an advanced feature allowing Fighters to gain proficiency or enhanced benefits with particular weapon types, unlocking specialized maneuvers or combat techniques.

7. **Interaction with Bloodline Powers**: 
   - Fighters can access and tap into their bloodline powers at certain levels, potentially enhancing their martial capabilities (e.g., temporary bonuses, unique combat abilities, or elemental enhancements).
   - Bloodline powers may influence fighting styles or archetypes, offering tailored synergies based on their ancestry.

## Design Ideas

- **Bloodline-Infused Combat Styles**: Integrate fighting styles with bloodline powers. For instance, a Fighter with a fire-oriented bloodline might gain additional damage or effects with fire-based weapon attacks.

- **Tactical Maneuvers**: Leverage archetypes that focus on tactical manipulation of battlefield positioning. Skills like combat maneuvers could incorporate bloodline traits to create unique tactical advantages.

- **Adaptive Resilience**: Enhance Second Wind with bloodline traits, allowing Fighters to also gain temporary resistances or bonuses based on their lineage.

- **Dynamic Action Surge**: Create unique variants of Action Surge that interact with bloodline powers to allow for diverse tactical choices (e.g., additional movement away from an enemy affected by a psychic bloodline).

## Possible Implementation Notes

- **Balancing**: Ensure that bloodline powers serve to enhance and not overshadow the Fighter's core mechanic. Careful balancing is essential to maintain gameplay fairness.

- **Level Progression**: Structure the level progression to ensure that Fighters continually feel empowered and adaptable, with opportunities to evolve their fighting style and bloodline synergies.

- **Playtesting**: Emphasize thorough playtesting of different builds (various archetypes with varying bloodline abilities) to identify potential imbalance or synergy issues.

- **Lore Integration**: Incorporate lore behind bloodlines into the design of fighting styles and archetypes to create a rich narrative experience, building on the themes of ancestry, legacy, and personal identity. 

By considering these elements, the Fighter class in BLOODLINES RPG can be designed to be powerful, versatile, and deeply rooted in the game's broader themes, culminating in a satisfying experience for players who enjoy martial combat and character customization.
