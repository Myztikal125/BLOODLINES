# BLOODLINES RPG Wizard Class Design Research Notes

## Summary
In the BLOODLINES RPG, the Wizard class is designed to embody a master of arcane knowledge, drawing upon the deep well of magic through intense study and practice. Unlike other spellcasting classes, Wizards rely heavily on their intellect and have a unique relationship with bloodline powers. This class is characterized by intricate spell mechanics, allowing for diverse play styles and interactions with the game world.

## Important Mechanics

1. **Spell Progression**
   - Wizards gain access to spells as they level up. Each spell level unlocks new capabilities, where higher-level spells require greater skill and knowledge.
   - At every even-numbered level, Wizards can learn additional spells from their spellbook.

2. **Spell Slots**
   - Wizards utilize spell slots to cast spells, with slots increasing in usage as they gain levels. 
   - Slots are replenished on a long rest, allowing for strategic planning in adventuring days.

3. **Arcane Traditions**
   - Wizards choose an Arcane Tradition at level 1, granting them unique abilities and shaping their magical focus (e.g., Elementalism, Illusion, Conjuration).
   - Each Tradition provides specialized spells and perks at certain levels.

4. **Cantrips**
   - Wizards have access to a number of cantrips (0-level spells) that can be cast without using spell slots, providing versatility in non-combat situations.
   - Cantrips grow in potency based on the Wizard’s level.

5. **Ritual Casting**
   - Wizards can cast spells as rituals without expending a spell slot but take longer to cast.
   - Specific spells are designated as ritual spells, allowing for flexible spellcasting outside of combat.

6. **Spellbook Mechanics**
   - Wizards maintain a spellbook that contains the spells they have learned. 
   - Spells can be copied into the spellbook from scrolls or other spellbooks at the cost of time and materials.
   - A Wizard's spellbook can also be a source of personal flavor, containing notes and additional lore.

7. **Intelligence-Based Casting**
   - Wizards use Intelligence as their primary ability for spellcasting, influencing spell DCs and attack rolls.
   - This feature encourages players to invest in Intelligence for increased effectiveness in their spells.

8. **Interaction with Bloodline Powers**
   - Wizards can integrate their arcane knowledge with bloodline traits, unlocking unique spell variations or enhanced effects based on their bloodline (e.g., a draconic bloodline might enhance fire spells).

## Design Ideas

- **Spell Variability:** Allow spells to have different effects or modes depending on caster level or specific Arcane Tradition choice.
- **Dynamic Spell Learning:** Introduce a mechanic where Wizards can learn spells from defeated foes, representing the nature of their understanding over time.
- **Bloodline Synergy:** Create mechanics where certain Wizard spells can amplify or enhance the effects of bloodline powers, leading to hybridized magical effects.
- **Personal Spells:** Encourage players to create unique spells through a mentorship system, where Wizards can petition their GM to craft new spells that fit within the game world’s lore.

## Possible Implementation Notes

- **Balancing Spell Slots:**
  Ensure that the number of spell slots at different levels balances the power of spellcasting, considering the inclusion of high-damage or control spells at higher levels.
  
- **Spellbook Customization:**
  Allow players the freedom to add personal notes, lore, or sketches to their spellbooks, making them feel more invested in their character's story.
  
- **Character Backstory Integration:**
  Consider including a character development mechanic that rewards wizards for their backgrounds and the study of magic, such as gaining access to ancient tomes or unlocking secret spells related to their backstory.
  
- **Playtesting:**
  Extensive playtesting of spell balance, Arcane Traditions, and bloodline interactions will be crucial to ensure that Wizards maintain unique capabilities without overshadowing other classes.

By focusing on these structures and mechanics, the Wizard class in BLOODLINES RPG will present players with a rich and engaging experience, marrying the elements of magical mastery with the intricate stories of heritage and bloodlines.
