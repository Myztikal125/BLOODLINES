# Rogue Class Design for BLOODLINES RPG

## Summary
The Rogue class in BLOODLINES RPG is designed to excel in stealth, cunning, and agility, utilizing both physical skills and supernatural abilities that may stem from various bloodlines. The class embodies themes of deception, agility, and quick thinking, making Rogues versatile in combat and exploration. Central mechanics include Sneak Attack, Cunning Action, the use of Thieves' Tools, and various roguish archetypes that further define their capabilities. Rogues also possess unique defensive abilities like Evasion and Uncanny Dodge, which allow them to navigate hazardous situations effectively. The interaction of Rogues with inherited bloodline powers adds a layer of customization and versatility to their gameplay.

## Important Mechanics

### Sneak Attack
- **Functionality:** Allows Rogues to deal extra damage under specific conditions, such as when the target is distracted or flanked, simulating a tactical advantage.
- **Scaling:** The damage increases as the character levels up, rewarding skill and investment in the class.
- **Requirements:** Typically, the Rogue must have advantage on the attack roll or an ally must be within 5 feet of the target.

### Cunning Action
- **Utility:** Enables Rogues to use their bonus action to Hide, Dash, or Disengage, enhancing their mobility and tactical positioning.
- **Strategic Play:** This mechanic encourages careful planning and advantageous positioning, allowing Rogues to escape danger or surprise enemies.

### Thieves’ Tools
- **Skills:** Rogues gain expertise in the use of Thieves' Tools, enabling them to effectively disarm traps and open locks.
- **Roleplay Opportunities:** This mechanic opens up creative problem-solving opportunities, expanding a Rogue’s role beyond combat.

### Roguish Archetypes
- **Customization:** Rogues can choose from various archetypes (e.g., Assassin, Arcane Trickster, Swashbuckler) that grant specific abilities and thematic powers, tailoring the character to the player's playstyle.
- **Bloodline Interaction:** Archetypes may be influenced or enhanced by specific bloodline powers, introducing synergy between class choices and character background.

### Evasion
- **Defensive Mechanic:** Allows Rogues to take no damage on a successful Dexterity saving throw against area effects (e.g., dragon breath, fireballs).
- **Level Requirement:** Typically acquired at later levels, emphasizing the Rogue’s agility and quick reflexes.

### Uncanny Dodge
- **Survivability:** Enables Rogues to use their reaction to halve damage from an attack they can see.
- **Tactical Diverse:** This ability encourages Rogues to engage directly in combat while maintaining survivability.

### Bloodline Interaction
- **Synergistic Powers:** Rogues benefit from unique bloodline powers, enhancing skills related to deception, manipulation, or agility.
- **Crossover Mechanics:** Consider developing a system where specific bloodlines provide bonuses to Sneak Attack damage or expand utility options for Cunning Action.

## Design Ideas
1. **Bloodline Synergy:**
   - Design specific roguish archetypes that are closely tied with particular bloodlines. For example, a "Shadowed Rogue" archetype that gains powers enhancing stealth rooted in a shadowy or dark bloodline.

2. **Crafting Mechanics:**
   - Incorporate a crafting system where Rogues can create or upgrade Thieves’ Tools or weapons using materials found during adventures, allowing more character customization and depth.

3. **Spy Network:**
   - Develop a system whereby Rogues can establish and leverage a network of informants, gaining insights and additional opportunities based on interactions through their background.

4. **Bloodline-Infused Abilities:**
   - Create bloodline powers that can be activated in stealth situations, granting temporary advantages or unique effects during Sneak Attacks or during Cunning Action moments.

## Possible Implementation Notes
- **Balance Concerns:** Ensure that the combination of Sneak Attack damage, bloodline powers, and roguish archetype abilities do not become overpowering in combat scenarios. Regular playtesting is critical to maintain balance.

- **Narrative Integration:** Work alongside narrative designers to incorporate unique story arcs that explore the interplay between a Rogue's skills and their bloodline heritage, enriching the overall roleplaying experience.

- **Player Agency:** Build mechanics that encourage creativity and adaptability, empowering players to utilize the Rogue’s skillset effectively in various scenarios, both in combat and roleplay.

- **Flexible Design:** Consider making some aspects of the Rogue class modular to allow players to adjust their character’s effectiveness based on their chosen bloodline and preferred playstyle, enhancing their engagement and investment in their character. 

By focusing on these mechanics and ideas, the Rogue class can bring a unique flavor to the BLOODLINES RPG, emphasizing the interplay of skillful play and bloodline heritage.
