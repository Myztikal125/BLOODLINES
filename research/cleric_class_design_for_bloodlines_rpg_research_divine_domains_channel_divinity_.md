## BLOODLINES RPG: Cleric Class Design Research Notes

### Summary
The Cleric class in BLOODLINES RPG centers on a character’s connection to divine powers, drawing from celestial, primordial, or other divine sources. Clerics serve as conduits of faith, possessing abilities to heal, protect, and channel divine energy. In the BLOODLINES systems, Clerics can also interact with bloodline powers, creating unique synergies and diverse character builds.

### Important Mechanics

1. **Divine Domains:**
   - Clerics select a Divine Domain that reflects their patron deity's influence, such as Healing, War, Protection, or Trickery. Each domain grants unique class features and spells.
   - Domains grant access to specific spells and abilities that enhance clerical flexibility.

2. **Channel Divinity:**
   - A core mechanic allowing clerics to manifest their deity's will. This power can be used for various effects, including turning undead or bolstering allies.
   - Possible uses could include healing, bestowing buffs, or inflicting damage.

3. **Spell Preparation:**
   - Clerics prepare spells from their spell list daily, offering adaptability based on the challenges they anticipate.
   - Depending on mechanics for spell slots, preparation may be linked to the chosen domain, providing clerics with domain-specific spells available for preparation.

4. **Turn Undead:**
   - A signature cleric ability enabling the cleric to repel or destroy undead creatures using divine power.
   - This feature can scale with levels or tie to the cleric’s alignment and deity's portfolio, increasing in effectiveness as the cleric gains experience.

5. **Divine Intervention:**
   - A powerful ability that allows clerics to call upon their deity to intervene directly in critical moments.
   - It may involve making a prayer check, and if successful, the deity can provide aid or miraculous outcomes.

6. **Bloodline Interaction:**
   - Clerics may possess bloodline powers influenced by their divine patron. These powers could include unique augmentations or transformations reflecting the cleric's heritage, allowing for diverse builds.
   - Specific synergies could be explored, where bloodline traits enhance or modify cleric features (e.g., a bloodline that enhances healing spells).

### Design Ideas

1. **Diverse Domains:**
   - Create unique Divine Domains that resonate with different cultures, mythologies, and philosophies within the BLOODLINES setting. This could introduce new playable options and enrich character backstories.

2. **Modified Channel Divinity:**
   - Introduce versatile uses for Channel Divinity that can be customized based on the cleric’s environment or party needs.
   - Consider a choice system, allowing players to specialize their Channel Divinity effects as they level up.

3. **Bloodline Infusion:**
   - Introduce classes that can choose to anchor themselves not only in divine sources but also tap into their bloodline powers. This could include the option to hybridize spells or abilities that draw from both domains.

4. **Customizable Spell Lists:**
   - Allow players to influence their spell lists with the chosen domain and incorporate bloodline elements that provide thematic spells that speak to their unique heritage.

5. **Story-driven Divine Intervention:**
   - Make Divine Intervention not only a mechanical feature but also a narrative tool that can serve as a plot driver. The outcome can tie into the character's backstory, potentially influencing the campaign's direction.

### Possible Implementation Notes

- **Playtesting:** Establish a series of playtest sessions to evaluate the balance and functionality of the cleric class, especially how domain abilities and bloodline powers interact in practice.
  
- **Integration of Lore:** Tie cleric abilities and domains into the broader lore of the BLOODLINES world. Ensure that deities are richly developed to complement the gameplay.

- **Character Progression:** Design a leveling system that allows clerics to progressively unlock divine powers, bloodline abilities, and domains, encouraging players to explore their character’s growth over time.

- **Feedback Loop:** Create a mechanism for players to provide feedback on their clerics’ performance, allowing iterative improvements and further balance adjustments post-launch.

By considering these aspects, BLOODLINES RPG can offer a robust and captivating Cleric class that enriches the player experience while staying true to the game's core themes.
