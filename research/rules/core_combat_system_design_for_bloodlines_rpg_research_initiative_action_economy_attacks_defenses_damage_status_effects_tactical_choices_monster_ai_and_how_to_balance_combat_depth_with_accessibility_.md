# Summary
The core combat system for BLOODLINES RPG aims to create a balance between tactical depth and accessibility. This involves analyzing existing combat systems in RPGs, particularly focusing on initiative, action economy, attack and defense mechanics, damage calculations, status effects, and enemy AI behavior. Additionally, the integration of tactical choices and ensuring balanced gameplay will be crucial to enhancing player engagement.

# Existing Systems
1. **Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) 5e**
   - **Initiative:** Roll for initiative at start of combat.
   - **Action Economy:** Players typically take one action, one bonus action (if applicable), and move.
   - **Attacks & Defenses:** Roll to hit (Attack Roll) against a target's Armor Class (AC).
   - **Damage:** Variable damage based on weapon and dice (e.g., d10 for greatsword).
   - **Status Effects:** Conditions (e.g., stunned, poisoned) provide strategic depth.
   - **Monster AI:** Often follows predefined behaviors or simple rules.

2. **Final Fantasy Tactics**
   - **Initiative:** Turn order based on speed stats.
   - **Action Economy:** Players can perform an action (attack, cast a spell) or move.
   - **Attacks & Defenses:** Directly linked to character stats and abilities.
   - **Damage:** Calculated based on character abilities and enemy weaknesses.
   - **Status Effects:** Include various debuffs that affect character actions.
   - **Monster AI:** AI capable of optimizing actions based on tactical board position.

3. **Darkest Dungeon**
   - **Initiative:** Turn order based on speed; actions can disrupt opponents.
   - **Action Economy:** Players can choose actions, with some having additional costs (stress, positioning).
   - **Attacks & Defenses:** Emphasizes positioning for advantages (front-line vs. back-line).
   - **Damage:** RNG-based with potential for critical hits.
   - **Status Effects:** Heavy use of stress and bleed mechanics create tension.
   - **Monster AI:** AI adapts to player strategies using various tactical setups.

# Important Mechanics
- **Initiative & Turn Order:** Establish a fair turn order that allows characters and enemies to react strategically.
- **Action Economy:** Determine how actions, bonus actions, and movements interact, encouraging tactical decision-making.
- **Damage Calculation:** Incorporate variability and critical hits to create excitement; consider flat vs. randomized damage.
- **Status Effects:** Develop a range of conditions that affect combat dynamics while ensuring players understand their implications.
- **Tactical Choices:** Allow players to make meaningful decisions, such as positioning, target selection, and resource management.
- **Monster AI:** Create varied AI strategies that adapt to player choices, promoting a dynamic combat experience.

# Strengths
- Encourages player engagement through strategic decision-making.
- Potential for deep, varied combat scenarios with diverse character builds.
- Strong narrative integration through status effects and combat consequences.
- Can provide both casual and hardcore players a rewarding experience.

# Weaknesses
- Complexity might overwhelm new players, leading to unbalanced participation.
- Balancing combat depth while maintaining clarity in rules can be challenging.
- Randomness in damage calculation can frustrate tactical planning.
- AI complexity might lead to inconsistent enemy behavior, reducing engagement.

# BLOODLINES Ideas
- **Dynamic Initiative System:** Introduce mechanics that allow players to influence turn order (e.g., actions could gain "initiative points").
- **Action Economy Variability:** Consider energy or stamina systems where different actions consume varying amounts of resources.
- **Conditional Status Effects:** Create effects that can improve or worsen based on in-game conditions (e.g., weather impacts fire spells).
- **Action Synergy:** Develop abilities that enhance others when used in conjunction, promoting teamwork and strategy.
- **Adaptive Monster AI:** Implement basic learning where monsters alter their strategies based on player tendencies in combat.

# Implementation Notes
- Ensure clear and concise explanations of mechanics to support player understanding.
- Create a tutorial system that walks players through complex mechanics at their own pace.
- Focus on iterative gameplay testing to find the right balance in combat depth vs. accessibility.
- Consider integrating visual aids (e.g. icons for status effects) to streamline gameplay.

# Questions For Lead Designer
1. What level of complexity do we want to offer in the combat system to ensure it’s accessible yet engaging?
2. How should we balance action economy against strategic options for players?
3. Should we focus on randomization effects or establish a more predictable damage system?
4. Are there specific status effects we want to prioritize in terms of narrative or gameplay impact?
5. How much variability in monster AI should we include to enhance player experience without compromising clarity?
