# Summary
The Bloodlines system in the BLOODLINES RPG can be a foundational aspect of character identity and progression. By incorporating ancestry, inherited powers, and various mechanics like mutations and curses, the Bloodline system can deeply influence gameplay and storytelling. This research outlines existing systems and proposes concepts for a rich, engaging Bloodline mechanic.

# Existing Systems
1. **Vampire: The Masquerade (VTM)**
   - Bloodlines tied to vampiric clans (Clans have unique abilities).
   - Disciplines (supernatural powers) based on clan heritage.
   - Weaknesses corresponding to bloodlines, affecting gameplay.

2. **Dungeons & Dragons (D&D)**
   - Racial features that influence character class and abilities.
   - Option for multi-classing to blend skills and powers.
   - Feats and custom lineage allowing tailoring of bloodline traits.

3. **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay**
   - Strong emphasis on ancestry and background.
   - Randomly determined mutations and flaws as character growth elements.
   - Ancestral traits can influence character story arcs.

4. **Mage: The Ascension**
   - Magic and bloodline interact to affect a character’s capabilities.
   - Various Traditions derive powers from their magical heritage.

# Important Mechanics
- **Inheritance of Powers**: Bloodlines can grant abilities that can evolve or become stronger over time.
- **Progression Systems**: Systems of leveling up tied to bloodline themes (e.g., certain actions or requirements to unlock new powers).
- **Awakening Mechanics**: Events or milestones that allow players to enhance or discover new abilities tied to their lineage.
- **Mutations/Curses**: Random or player-directed developments that can provide both benefits and drawbacks.
- **Blessings**: Positive traits or bonuses as a consequence of bloodline heritage, enhancing character abilities or story interactions.

# Strengths
- **Deep Character Identity**: Bloodlines could provide a rich backstory, impacting how characters interact with the world and others.
- **Dynamic Progression**: Characters adapt and grow in ways that reflect their heritage, offering diverse gameplay experiences.
- **Interpersonal Drama**: Different bloodlines can create conflict or alliance opportunities between characters, enriching the narrative.
- **Diverse Play Styles**: Players can design their characters around preferred mechanics (e.g., stealth, combat, magic) informed by their bloodline.

# Weaknesses
- **Balancing Issues**: Different bloodlines can lead to significant disparities in character effectiveness.
- **Complexity**: An intricate system may overwhelm new players or complicate character creation.
- **Narrow Focus**: If too rigid, bloodline traits might lead to predictable or stale character development.

# BLOODLINES Ideas
1. **Dual Bloodlines**: Each character can inherit traits from two bloodlines, mixing abilities and creating unique combinations.
2. **Bloodline Evolution**: Characters can evolve their bloodline powers based on choices made in-game, reflecting growth and player agency.
3. **Legacy Quests**: Special quests tied to a character's bloodline that unlock new powers or further deepen their lineage lore.
4. **Bloodline Curses**: Players can opt to accept curses for greater powers, creating moral dilemmas and significant narrative moments.

# Implementation Notes
- Create a clear framework for how bloodlines interact with other mechanics (e.g., skills, attributes).
- Develop guidelines to ensure bloodline traits remain balanced and offer a wide variety of flavor and mechanics.
- Consider art assets and lore to flesh out each bloodline, promoting rich storytelling and character engagement.
- Make bloodline choices impactful during character creation and throughout gameplay, allowing for revisiting and influencing story arcs.
