### BLOODLINES RPG: Ranger Class Design Research Notes

#### Summary
The Ranger class in BLOODLINES RPG embodies the harmony between wilderness survival and tactical prowess, offering a unique blend of martial skills, nature affinity, and spellcasting abilities. They are adept at traversing different terrains, utilizing animal companions, and harnessing their knowledge against favored enemies. Rangers in BLOODLINES can also weave their bloodline powers into their abilities, enhancing their versatility and effectiveness in both combat and exploration.

#### Important Mechanics

1. **Favored Enemy**: 
   - Rangers can select specific types or categories of creatures (e.g., undead, monstrous beasts, humanoids) to gain bonuses against them. This may include advantages on attack rolls, damage rolls, or skill checks related to tracking and hunting these foes.

2. **Natural Explorer**: 
   - This ability allows Rangers to be exceptionally skilled in certain terrains (e.g., forests, mountains, deserts). Benefits may include enhanced movement speed, natural camouflage, or bonuses on survival checks to find food or navigate.

3. **Ranger Archetypes**: 
   - Rangers can specialize into distinct archetypes. Possible archetypes include:
     - **Beastmaster**: Focuses on animal companions and agility.
     - **Spellbound Hunter**: Centers on spellcasting and elements to enhance combat.
     - **Tracker**: Specializes in stealth and tracking, focusing on ambush tactics.

4. **Spellcasting**: 
   - Rangers can learn a limited number of nature-themed spells (e.g., healing, summoning animals, enhancing physical attributes). Spell slots may vary based on level and archetype.

5. **Animal Companion**: 
   - Rangers can bond with a companion animal that aids them in combat and exploration. The companion’s abilities scale with the Ranger’s level, and they can serve both combat and utility roles.

6. **Bloodline Powers Interaction**: 
   - Rangers benefit from unique bloodline powers that can enhance their preferred combat style, tracking abilities, or connection with nature. Possible powers include increased spell potency, enhanced skills against favored enemies, or improved spirit animal affinity.

#### Design Ideas

1. **Hybrid Nature and Combat**: 
   - Rangers might have features that allow them to toggle between melee combat and spellcasting seamlessly, emphasizing versatility.

2. **Tracking and Lore Skills**: 
   - Consider granting Rangers additional Lore skills based on their favored enemies, allowing them to gather intel and strategize more effectively against threats.

3. **Enhanced Exploration**: 
   - Incorporate environmental challenges specific to each terrain, giving Rangers unique solutions or advantages during exploration.

4. **Multi-Functional Animal Companions**: 
   - Animal companions can be developed to allow for varied roles, such as a scout, a mount, or a combat aid, enhancing player strategy.

#### Possible Implementation Notes

1. **Progression System**: 
   - Define a clear progression path, outlining how favored enemy selection, terrain mastery, and animal companion bonding evolve as the Ranger levels up.

2. **Balanced Spell List**: 
   - Create a spell list that reflects the Ranger’s nature and survivability theme, ensuring spells don't overlap too heavily with other spellcasting classes.

3. **Bloodline Variants**: 
   - Introduce bloodline variants that affect Ranger abilities, e.g., a Celestial Ranger could have spells with healing properties while a Fiendish Ranger might have enhanced combat against celestial creatures.

4. **Class Synergy**: 
   - Encourage interactions with other classes to promote team synergy—perhaps Rangers gain advantages when working with Druids or Fighters, providing tactical advantages in combat situations.

By considering these components, the Ranger class can be effectively integrated into the BLOODLINES RPG, creating a unique and engaging playstyle that enhances both individual character strategy and group dynamics.
