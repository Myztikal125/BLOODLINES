# BLOODLINES Wizard Magic System Research

## **Summary**
The magic system in BLOODLINES aims to provide a unique and customizable experience for players, influenced by various established systems from both D&D and Pathfinder. The goal is to blend the classic appeal of spellcasting with innovative mechanics that enhance both gameplay and player engagement. 

## **Existing System Comparisons**

1. **D&D Wizard Spell Progression** 
   - *Mechanics*: Wizards gain spell slots as they level up and can prepare spells from a spellbook.
   - *Strengths*: Versatile; players can adapt their spells daily based on needs.
   - *Weaknesses*: Heavy reliance on preparation can hinder spontaneous play.

2. **Pathfinder Spell Systems**
   - *Mechanics*: Similar to D&D but with additional flexibility in spellcasting and a wider variety of spells.
   - *Strengths*: More options for customization and greater control over spellcasting.
   - *Weaknesses*: Complexity can overwhelm new players.

3. **Spell Preparation vs. Known Spells**
   - *Preparation Systems*: Require pre-selection of spells, promoting strategic planning.
   - *Known Spells Systems*: Allow for a set list of spells that can be used at will; fosters adaptability.
   - *Strengths of Preparation*: Encourages strategic gameplay.
   - *Strengths of Known Spells*: Enhances player freedom and adaptability.

4. **Mana/Resource-Based Magic Systems**
   - *Mechanics*: Magic points (mana) are expended to cast spells. Resources regenerate over time.
   - *Strengths*: Streamlines spellcasting and allows for more fluid gameplay.
   - *Weaknesses*: Could lead to frustrations if mana management is overly punishing.

5. **Spell Schools and Customization**
   - *Schools*: Usually categorize spells into themes (e.g., Evocation, Illusion).
   - *Customization*: Systems that allow players to modify spells for unique effects.
   - *Strengths*: Encourage diverse character builds.
   - *Weaknesses*: Balancing school strengths and weaknesses can be challenging.

6. **Spell Crafting**
   - *Mechanics*: Allows players to create custom spells with unique attributes.
   - *Strengths*: Empowering for players and fosters creativity.
   - *Weaknesses*: Complex and risks breaking balance if not properly managed.

7. **Balancing Magic Users**
   - *Considerations*: Ensuring magic users are powerful but not overly dominant in gameplay; potential for restrictions based on game context.
   - *Strengths*: Provides challenge and encourages party diversity.
   - *Weaknesses*: Overbalancing can frustrate players who enjoy playing spellcasters.

8. **AI Generated Magic Systems**
   - *Innovative Approaches*: Using AI for random generation of spells or adapting existing spells on-the-fly.
   - *Strengths*: Unlimited creativity potential and dynamic content generation. 
   - *Weaknesses*: Consistency and coherence issues may arise.

## **Strengths and Weaknesses**

### Strengths
- **Flexibility**: Customizable spells and choices make for engaging and varied gameplay.
- **Strategy**: A system that encourages planning and tactical decision-making.
- **Creativity**: Opportunities for unique spell crafting to appeal to imaginative players.

### Weaknesses
- **Complexity**: Potential overwhelming nature for new players if too many systems are integrated.
- **Balance Issues**: Maintaining parity between classes while providing unique flavor can be difficult.

## **Recommended BLOODLINES Mechanics**

1. **Hybrid Spellcasting System**: Combine preparation and known spells. Players can select a few prepared spells each day while also having a set number of known spells for spontaneous casting.
2. **Mana System**: Implement a mana/resource-based system with varied recovery rates or techniques (like resting, meditating, or consuming items).
3. **Spell Schools with Unique Mechanics**: Introduce schools that offer distinct advantages or mechanics, fostering specialization.
4. **Spell Crafting & Customization Options**: Allow players to tweak spells to create unique effects through modular components (like adding an elemental effect or a utility component).
5. **Balance through Variation**: Introduce limits for powerful spells while giving casters tools to excel in different circumstances like environmental advantages.

## **Implementation Ideas**

- **Character Creation**: Offer a variety of spell origins that can influence spell styles and abilities; allowing players to choose different paths for their wizard.
- **Progression and Milestones**: Design progression systems that reward players for museums and crafting unique spells, and introduce milestones for gaining new abilities.
- **Spell Toolkits**: Provide players with a toolkit for crafting spells that includes a range of elements, school options, and modifiers to enable creativity.
- **Playtesting**: Conduct extensive playtesting to fine-tune balance and complexity.

## **Questions for Lead Designer**
1. How do you envision the balance of spellcasters relative to other classes?
2. What flavor or theme do you want the BLOODLINES magic system to convey?
3. Do you have any preferences for the method of spell acquisition (preparing spells vs. learning new spells)?
4. How complex do you want the crafting and customization mechanics to be?
5. Should the system include elements of randomness or unpredictability, or would you prefer all mechanics to be deterministic?
