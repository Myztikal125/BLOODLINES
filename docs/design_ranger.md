# Design Decision Document: Ranger Class for BLOODLINES RPG

## Approved Concepts

1. **Favored Enemy**: 
   - Rangers select specific creature types for combat bonuses (attack rolls, damage, tracking).
  
2. **Natural Explorer**:
   - Ability to excel in certain terrains, gaining benefits such as enhanced movement, camouflage, and survival skill bonuses.
  
3. **Ranger Archetypes**:
   - Implementation of three distinct archetypes: 
     - **Beastmaster**
     - **Spellbound Hunter**
     - **Tracker**
  
4. **Spellcasting**: 
   - Limited number of nature-themed spells available based on level and archetype.
  
5. **Animal Companion**: 
   - A bond with an animal that aids both in combat and exploration, gaining abilities that scale with the Ranger's level.
  
6. **Bloodline Powers Interaction**:
   - Inclusion of bloodline powers enhancing the Ranger’s combat style, tracking, and nature affinity.
  
7. **Hybrid Nature and Combat Mechanics**:
   - Features enabling seamless toggling between melee and spellcasting.
  
8. **Enhanced Exploration Mechanics**:
   - Environmental challenges tied to terrains that Rangers can overcome uniquely.

## Rejected Concepts

1. **Overemphasis on Spellcasting**:
   - Rangers will not be designed to compete with full spellcasting classes concerning the number and potency of spells.
  
2. **Unrestricted Animal Companion Selection**:
   - Limitations on animal companions will remain to ensure balance and clarity of roles.

3. **Specialized Skills Tied to each Terrain**:
   - While terrain mastery exists, overly complex specialized skills unique to every terrain will be avoided for streamlined gameplay.

## Required Changes

1. **Balanced Spell List Development**:
   - Ensure spells are distinctly nature-themed and do not overlap significantly with other classes. Aim for a unique flavor tied to each archetype.
  
2. **Clarify Progression System**:
   - Establish detailed advancement mechanics for favored enemy choices, terrain mastery, and animal companion growth across levels.

3. **Bloodline Variants Expansion**:
   - Elaborate on Bloodline Variants with specific examples that can influence Ranger abilities, ensuring diversity in playstyle without compromising balance.

4. **Class Synergy Mechanics**:
   - Develop specific mechanics that encourage teamwork with other classes, ensuring that the Ranger can provide tactical benefits when paired with specific allies.

## Implementation Guidance

1. **Playtesting Required**:
   - Conduct playtesting sessions focused on the Ranger class to assess balance and functionality of mechanics, especially favored enemies, spellcasting, and animal companions.

2. **Documentation**:
   - Create thorough reference materials outlining Ranger features, rules, and interactions with bloodline powers and other classes.

3. **Community Feedback Loop**:
   - Involve the community for feedback on Ranger mechanics, especially regarding their integration with different bloodline powers.

## Future Questions

1. **How can Ranger’s unique attributes promote strategic depth in group dynamics?**
  
2. **What specific nature-themed spells will provide unique advantages without overshadowing other classes?**
  
3. **How will the progression system accommodate multi-classed Rangers, especially regarding favored enemy and animal companion mechanics?**
  
4. **What limitations should we impose on animal companions to maintain balance without making them feel insignificant to players?**

By considering these comprehensive decisions, changes, and future queries, the Ranger class can be effectively designed and implemented into the BLOODLINES RPG, providing a dynamic and engaging experience for players.
