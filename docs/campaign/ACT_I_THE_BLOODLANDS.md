# BLOODLINES Campaign — Act I: The Bloodlands Awaken

## Campaign Role

This document is the production plan for the opening playable campaign arc. It is content, not a new rules system. Existing BLOODLINES combat, quest, NPC, progression, bloodline, and save systems remain authoritative.

## Opening Hook

Shadow begins in Ashenvale after rumors spread that scholar Lyra Vey disappeared while investigating ancient bloodline records. Elder Varen knows the scholar was looking for a connection between dormant bloodline powers and an older power buried beneath the forest.

## Act I Arc

### Chapter 1 — The Lost Scholar
- Start in Ashenvale.
- Discover the Ruined Watchtower.
- Investigate Lyra's research.
- Follow the hidden passage beneath the ruins.
- Find evidence of Lyra's disappearance and the first Bloodglass clue.

### Chapter 2 — The Hollowroot Trail
- Follow the trail from the watchtower into the Hollowroot Path.
- Encounter creatures drawn toward the same magical signal.
- Meet Warden Marek.
- Recover a missing page from Lyra's research.
- Learn that the trail points toward Blackspire Keep.

### Chapter 3 — Secrets of Blackspire
- Reach Blackspire Keep.
- Meet Kora Ironhand and learn the fortress history.
- Explore the sealed archive.
- Recover the Bloodglass Key.
- Discover that the key was created to open an ancient sanctum.

### Chapter 4 — The Bloodglass Sanctum
- Reach the Bloodglass Sanctum.
- Meet Keeper Serah.
- Confront the sanctum guardian.
- Open the Bloodglass archives.
- Discover the first major truth about the awakening bloodlines.
- End Act I with the campaign mystery established for Act II.

## Recurring Characters

- **Elder Varen** — Ashenvale keeper and first source of the campaign mystery.
- **Lyra Vey** — missing scholar whose research drives the opening arc.
- **Warden Marek** — wilderness ally who helps interpret the Hollowroot trail.
- **Kora Ironhand** — Blackspire smith with knowledge of the fortress and its history.
- **Keeper Serah** — guardian of the Bloodglass Sanctum.

## Player Experience Target

Act I should provide:

- Exploration across multiple locations.
- Multiple quest objectives and rewards.
- NPC interaction and recurring characters.
- Combat encounters tied to the story.
- Character progression.
- Bloodline-related mystery and discovery.
- A clear conclusion that leads into Act II.

## Production Rule

Do not invent new combat mechanics to support this campaign. When existing mechanics are insufficient, treat the missing behavior as an implementation task only when it is already approved by the Rules Bible; otherwise bring the specific design decision to the Lead Designer.
