# Design Decision Document for BLOODLINES RPG Wizard Class

## Design Decision
The Wizard class design is officially approved with the outlined mechanics focusing on arcane mastery, intelligence-based casting, and the integration of bloodline powers. This approach emphasizes strategic spellcasting, customization, and player agency.

## Approved Concepts
1. **Spell Progression & Slots**: Wizards unlock spells at each level, with increased spell slots as they progress, replenished on long rests.
2. **Arcane Traditions**: Wizards may choose an Arcane Tradition at level 1 which provides unique abilities and spells specific to their chosen path.
3. **Cantrips & Ritual Casting**: Access to cantrips for versatile gameplay and the ability to cast specific spells as rituals without using spell slots.
4. **Spellbook Mechanics**: Wizards maintain a customizable spellbook which can include unique spells, notes, and lore.
5. **Intelligence-Based Casting**: Spellcasting effectiveness is tied to Intelligence, promoting strategic character building.
6. **Bloodline Interaction**: Wizards can enhance spells based on their bloodline, creating unique magical effects.
7. **Dynamic Spell Learning**: Wizards can learn spells from defeated foes, allowing for ongoing development of their spell repertoire.

## Rejected Concepts
1. **Overcomplicated Spell Customization**: Any proposals that overly convolute spellcasting or spell learning mechanics (e.g., excessive complexity with spell components) have been rejected.
2. **Mandatory Spell Rituals**: Spells being mandatory to cast as rituals to use spell slots were dismissed as this could hinder gameplay dynamics.
3. **Limitations on Cantrip Usage**: Proposals to limit the number of cantrips a Wizard can have at any time were rejected to maintain versatility in gameplay.

## Required Changes
1. **Spell Variability Implementation**: More clarity is needed on how spell variability will be balanced fairly. Specific effects must be documented and modified based on caster level.
2. **Balancing Future Playtests**: Ensure that the distinguished features of Arcane Traditions are balanced during playtesting to avoid overshadowing other classes.
3. **Customization Guidelines for Spellbooks**: Introduce clearer parameters around what players can add to their spellbooks to maintain balance while encouraging personal investment.

## Implementation Guidance
- **Playtesting Schedule**: Develop a phased playtesting plan to focus on key areas including spell balance, Arcane Traditions, and bloodline interactions. Gather extensive player feedback for adjustments.
- **Incorporate Development Tools**: Create resources and tools for players to easily customize spells and spellbooks within guided limits.
- **Educational Materials**: Develop clear documentation for players to understand mechanics such as spell learning, casting, and bloodline interactions to ensure a smooth gameplay experience.

## Future Questions
1. How will the integration of bloodline powers into spellcasting specifically manifest in gameplay mechanics?
2. What methods can be employed to ensure that the learning of spells from foes has meaningful consequences on game dynamics?
3. How can the community provide feedback on the ongoing development of the Wizard class and its interactions with the game world? 
4. What is the optimal number of spells a Wizard should be able to learn per level to maintain challenge without creating a feeling of overwhelming choice? 

By addressing these points, the Wizard class in BLOODLINES RPG will provide a well-rounded and exciting experience for players, emphasizing both their mastery of magic and connection to their unique heritage.
