# Design Decision Document for BLOODLINES RPG: Fighter Class

## Design Decision
The Fighter class in BLOODLINES RPG will be designed to emphasize versatility in martial combat while intricately connecting to unique bloodline powers. Each Fighter will have an adaptable skill set based on their chosen fighting style, martial archetype, and lineage, ensuring a vibrant and personalized gameplay experience.

## Approved Concepts
1. **Action Surge**: Allows Fighters to take an additional action once per encounter, promoting tactical options in combat.
   
2. **Fighting Styles**: Fighters choose a preferred fighting style at certain levels, providing combat bonuses that reflect various approaches to martial combat.

3. **Martial Archetypes**: Three archetypes will be included—Champion, Battle Master, and Eldritch Knight—each offering distinct capabilities and synergies with bloodline traits.

4. **Second Wind**: Grants Fighters self-healing abilities to promote sustainability and aggressive combat tactics.

5. **Ability Score Improvements (ASI)**: Multiple opportunities for Fighters to enhance key abilities or select feats that enhance their combat effectiveness.

6. **Weapon Mastery**: Introduce an advanced feature allowing Fighters to unlock specialized techniques and proficiencies with specific weapon types.

7. **Interaction with Bloodline Powers**: Fighters can utilize unique bloodline powers that enhance combat capabilities, yielding tactical advantages tailored to their lineage.

8. **Bloodline-Infused Combat Styles**: Design combat styles influenced by bloodlines, such as elemental damage modifications and unique effects.

## Rejected Concepts
1. **Overly Complicated Power Combinations**: Avoid mechanics that intertwine bloodline powers and Fighter abilities excessively which could lead to confusion and imbalance.

2. **Displacement of Core Features**: Ensure that the integration of bloodline powers does not overshadow the inherent martial prowess that defines the Fighter class.

## Required Changes
1. **Balancing Mechanisms**: Implement careful balancing protocols to ensure that bloodline powers enhance but do not dominate Fighter mechanics. Establish a clear power scaling system that maintains fairness across classes.

2. **Level Progression Adjustment**: Refine the level progression to maintain a sense of empowerment at each tier. Each level should provide meaningful choices that reflect the evolving skills and story of the Fighter.

3. **Lore Connection**: Develop richer lore documentation on bloodlines influencing various Fighter aspects. This includes creating narratives that link lineage to fighting styles, archetypes, and special abilities.

## Implementation Guidance
1. **Design Iterations**: Create prototypes of Fighter abilities with integrated bloodline mechanics, leading to multiple iterations based on initial playtesting feedback.

2. **Playtesting Protocols**: Establish a diverse range of playtest groups focusing on different archetypes and bloodline interactions to uncover potential synergy issues and balance concerns.

3. **Player Feedback**: Gather qualitative data on players’ experiences with the Fighter class to identify areas for enhancement or rework post-launch.

## Future Questions
1. How can we further simplify the connection between bloodline powers and Fighter abilities to enhance player clarity without sacrificing depth?

2. What methods can be employed to ensure Fighters remain competitive against spellcasting or ranged combat classes in terms of versatility and power?

3. How do various bloodlines influence not just combat but the Fighter's narrative arc, and how can this be effectively translated into gameplay mechanics?

4. What additional archetypes might be necessary to achieve greater diversity, and how will they interact with the existing framework of bloodline powers? 

By adhering to these decisions, BLOODLINES RPG aims to establish a compelling Fighter class that resonates with player aspirations for customization, tactical complexity, and narrative engagement.
