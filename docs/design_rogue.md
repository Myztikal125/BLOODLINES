# Design Decision Document: Rogue Class for BLOODLINES RPG

## Approved Concepts
1. **Sneak Attack**
   - Implement the Sneak Attack mechanic as a core feature, allowing Rogues to deal extra damage under specific tactical conditions.

2. **Cunning Action**
   - Establish Cunning Action as a bonus action option, providing Rogues with enhanced mobility through Hide, Dash, or Disengage.

3. **Thieves’ Tools Proficiency**
   - Rogues will gain expertise in Thieves’ Tools to support their skills in disarming traps and opening locks, encouraging creative solutions in both combat and exploration.

4. **Roguish Archetypes**
   - Create distinct archetypes like Assassin, Arcane Trickster, and Swashbuckler, each offering unique abilities that synergize with bloodline powers.

5. **Evasion and Uncanny Dodge**
   - Include both Evasion and Uncanny Dodge to enhance the defensive capabilities of Rogues, reinforcing their agility theme in gameplay.

6. **Bloodline Interaction**
   - Develop specific bloodline powers that enhance or modify existing Rogue abilities, particularly focusing on stealth and deception.

## Rejected Concepts
1. **Extensive Crafting Mechanics**
   - The full crafting system for Thieves’ Tools and weapons will not be implemented to keep the focus on streamlined gameplay and character development; however, minor upgrades through quests may be considered.

2. **Spy Network System**
   - The development of a complex spy network is deemed too extensive for the initial release. Basic lore and background elements may be integrated, but not as a core mechanic.

3. **Extensive Bloodline-Infused Abilities**
   - While limited bloodline powers will be permitted, overly complex or numerous effects during Sneak Attacks or Cunning Actions will be avoided to maintain balance and clarity.

## Required Changes
1. **Balancing Mechanic Scaling**
   - Ensure that Sneak Attack scaling and bloodline bonuses do not cause power creep. This may involve setting caps and regular playtesting to monitor effectiveness.

2. **Narrative Elements**
   - Collaborate with narrative designers to integrate Rogue abilities with character-driven story arcs that emphasize the significance of bloodlines, ensuring a cohesive roleplaying experience.

3. **Player Agency & Customization**
   - Adjust modular class features for players to tailor their rogues to their preferred playstyle while protecting game balance.

## Implementation Guidance
- **Playtesting Phases**
  - Conduct regular playtests with focus groups to assess the Rogue class in various scenarios—combat, exploration, and social interactions—to identify balance issues.
  
- **Documentation**
  - Provide clear documentation for players regarding how bloodline powers interface with Rogue abilities, ensuring easy understanding of gameplay mechanics.

- **Feedback Loop**
  - Create channels for player feedback on the Rogue class mechanics, allowing iterative updates and improvements post-release.

## Future Questions
1. **What are the potential implications of balancing the interplay between Sneak Attack damage and bloodline powers across different gameplay scenarios?**
  
2. **How will we measure player engagement with the Rogue class over time, and what metrics will be indicative of successful integration of bloodline powers?**
  
3. **What additional content might be necessary to further enrich the Rogue experience post-launch, particularly in the realm of narrative and character development?**

By focusing on these structured decisions and establishing clear guidelines, we can create a compelling and balanced Rogue class that enhances the overall experience of BLOODLINES RPG while allowing for player creativity and character investment.
