# Design Decision Document: Cleric Class in BLOODLINES RPG

## Design Decision
To create a compelling and balanced Cleric class that emphasizes divine connection, versatility, and the integration of bloodline powers, thereby enriching gameplay and character development in the BLOODLINES RPG.

## Approved Concepts

1. **Divine Domains:**
   - Clerics will choose from a variety of Divine Domains that align with different deities and themes, each offering unique spells and class features to enhance character customization.
   
2. **Channel Divinity:**
   - Incorporate a versatile Channel Divinity mechanic with customizable uses based on cleric levels and chosen domains, allowing for situational adaptations by players.
   
3. **Spell Preparation:**
   - Retain the spell preparation system, which enhances adaptability and encourages strategic planning based on challenges faced by the party.
   
4. **Turn Undead:**
   - A core ability allowing clerics to interact with undead foes will be maintained and will scale with the cleric’s alignment and experience.
   
5. **Divine Intervention:**
   - Implement Divine Intervention as a narrative and mechanical tool, tying its outcomes to cleric backstories and the campaign's narrative arc.
   
6. **Bloodline Interaction:**
   - Allow clerics to possess bloodline powers that can enhance and modify cleric abilities, encouraging diverse character builds and adding depth to player choices.

## Rejected Concepts

1. **Overly Complex Channel Divinity Options:**
   - Avoid creating an excessive number of Channel Divinity options that could confuse players or disrupt game flow. Focus on quality and thematic relevance instead.
   
2. **Exclusively Divine Spell Lists:**
   - The idea of having a completely separate spell list that only reflected divine sources is rejected; instead, integrate bloodline influences into spell allocation.

3. **Static Domains:**
   - A proposal for static, unchanging domains was rejected in favor of dynamic domains that allow for flexibility and development over gameplay.

## Required Changes

1. **Design Unique Domain Features:**
   - Develop specific abilities and spells for each Divine Domain that reflect a coherent theme while ensuring they are balanced with one another.
   
2. **Narrative Integration for Divine Intervention:**
   - Enhance the consequences and narrative value of Divine Intervention to make it meaningful, ensuring it relates back to the character's backstory and is not merely a mechanical function.

3. **Balance Bloodline Powers:**
   - Ensure bloodline powers provide meaningful enhancements without overshadowing core cleric abilities, maintaining an equitable balance within the class.

## Implementation Guidance

- **Playtesting Plan:**
  - Conduct a series of focused playtest sessions to assess the functionality of cleric mechanics, especially the integration of bloodline powers and Unique Domain features.

- **Lore Development:**
  - Collaborate with the lore team to develop rich backstories for clerics’ patron deities and ties to the BLOODLINES setting, ensuring thematic coherence.

- **Character Progression:**
  - Create a streamlined leveling system that allows clerics to unlock new abilities and domains as they progress, reinforcing character development.

- **Feedback Mechanism:**
  - Establish regular feedback sessions during playtests to gather insights into players' experiences, allowing for iterative design improvements based on real players' input.

## Future Questions

1. **Domain Themes:**
   - What specific thematic elements and cultural influences should each Divine Domain draw from to enhance the BLOODLINES lore?

2. **Bloodline Synergies:**
   - How can we best balance the interaction between bloodline powers and divine abilities without creating overpowering combinations?

3. **Narrative Engagement:**
   - In what ways can we further integrate character backstories into mechanics for Divine Intervention to deepen player investment in their characters?

4. **Community Input:**
   - How do we intend to gather and implement community feedback post-launch to continually refine the cleric class as gameplay evolves?
